## 2. Uniforms in `shaders/brick_surface.gdshader`

| Uniform | Line | Type | Hint | Default | Group (`group_uniforms`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `albedo_tex` | 4 | `sampler2D` | `source_color, filter_linear_mipmap, repeat_enable` | none (sampler) | `colour` (line 3) |
| `albedo_tint` | 5 | `vec4` | `source_color` | `vec4(1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0)` | `colour` |
| `brightness` | 6 | `float` | `hint_range(0.0, 2.0)` | `1.0` | `colour` |
| `roughness_tex` | 9 | `sampler2D` | `filter_linear_mipmap, repeat_enable` | none (sampler) | `surface` (line 8) |
| `roughness_scale` | 10 | `float` | `hint_range(0.0, 1.0)` | `1.0` | `surface` |
| `uv_scale` | 11 | `float` | `hint_range(0.1, 4.0)` | `0.5` | `surface`; group closed at line 12 |

How they are used: `ALBEDO = texture(albedo_tex, uv).rgb * albedo_tint.rgb * brightness` (line 33);
`ROUGHNESS = texture(roughness_tex, uv).r * roughness_scale` (line 34). `uv` is the object-space
position (`VERTEX`, line 18) projected on the plane picked by the largest component of the
object-space normal (`NORMAL`, line 19): X → (z, −y) (lines 25–26), Y → (x, z) (lines 27–28),
otherwise Z → (x, −y) (lines 29–30), then times `uv_scale` (line 32). This matches the Revision
(box projection, X → ZY, Y → XZ, Z → XY; `uv_scale` 0.1–4.0, default 0.5).

`get_shader_uniform_list()` reports all six (log `logs/test_materials_60fps.log`, line "every source
uniform is in the compiled uniform list").

## 4. The light the walls are judged under

`Sun`, `DirectionalLight3D`, `scenes/asset_room_3d.tscn` lines 27–29:
- `transform = Transform3D(0.866, -0.383, 0.3214, 0, 0.6428, 0.766, -0.5, -0.6634, 0.5567, 0, 10, 0)`
  (line 28). The basis's Z column is (0.3214, 0.766, 0.5567); a directional light shines along its
  −Z, so light travels along (−0.321, −0.766, −0.557): from above (elevation about 50°), from the
  +x / +z side (the camera's side). Position (0, 10, 0) has no effect on a directional light.
- `shadow_enabled = true` (line 29). No other property is set, so colour, energy and the shadow
  mode are Godot's defaults (not read from the engine in this audit).
- It is the scene's only light; the scene has no `WorldEnvironment` (whole file, lines 1–61), so
  there is no SSAO at runtime.
- Facing: wall fronts (+z) get N·L ≈ 0.56, tops ≈ 0.77, +x ends ≈ 0.32; backs (−z) and −x ends
  face away. Compare the **front faces and tops**: by the same direction, CoverWall's shadow falls
  toward −x / −z and can reach CoverWall2's +x end face (lower part), but not its front or top.

## 6. Mismatches and open points

1. **Use External not set** for `M_Brick` (`cover_wall.glb.import` line 42 `_subresources={}`), so
   CoverWall still shows the flat imported colour. Human step in the editor.
2. **Hint PNG missing**: `assets/hint/grey_3x1.png` and its `.import` do not exist yet, so both hint
   materials and the quads' materials fail to load (test log). Human step.
3. Brief §5's **original** `uv_scale` row (`hint_range(0.5, 8.0)`, default 2.0) and "UVs scaled by
   `uv_scale`" differ from the shader (line 11: 0.1–4.0, 0.5; projection lines 23–32). The shader
   matches the **Revision**, which supersedes it. Not a defect.
4. Group names: brief §5 writes "Colour" / "Surface"; the shader uses `colour` / `surface`
   (lines 3, 8). The Inspector capitalises group names, so this is cosmetic only.
5. The brief says "SSAO off when judging roughness". At runtime the room has no environment, so
   that holds; in the **editor viewport** Godot adds its preview environment when a scene has no
   WorldEnvironment, which I did not inspect. Judge in the running scene (F6) or with the preview
   environment toggled off.
6. The two walls appear close together on screen (screen-x ranges touch, section 3). CoverWall2 is
   about 29 px from the left edge at 1280 × 720; a narrower editor viewport could crop it.
7. `scenes/asset_room_3d.tscn` already had uncommitted editor changes before this work (uid in the
   header, `unique_id` on every node, `load_steps` removed; `git diff`), apparently from an editor
   save. I left them as they were.
